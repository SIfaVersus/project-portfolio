# SYNTRA — Business Operating System

**Category:** Enterprise Software · Business Process Management  
**Status:** In Active Development  
**Year:** 2026 – Present

## Overview

SYNTRA is a business operating system designed for consulting firms, bringing key business operations together in a unified digital environment.

The project focuses on streamlining workflows, improving operational visibility and supporting structured business management.

## Key Features

- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Project and task management
- Financial data management
- Business process and workflow management
- Unified business management interface

## Technology Stack

**Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite  
**Backend:** C#, ASP.NET Core (.NET 8)  
**Database:** SQL Server

## Development Status

SYNTRA is under active development, with core business management modules implemented and further improvements ongoing.
