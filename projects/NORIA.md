# NORIA — AI-Powered Email Management System

**Category:** Business Process Automation · Artificial Intelligence  
**Status:** In Active Development  
**Year:** 2026 – Present

## Overview

NORIA is a desktop application for managing email campaigns, contacts and audience segmentation, with AI-assisted content creation and safeguards for responsible bulk email operations.

The project focuses on streamlining communication workflows while prioritising sender reputation, recipient consent and operational safety.

## Key Features

- Contact management and audience segmentation
- Campaign creation and scheduling
- Email templates and content editing
- AI-assisted content generation and rewriting
- Recipient consent and suppression management
- Sending limits and safety controls
- Campaign monitoring and operational audit records

## Technology Stack

**Desktop & UI:** Electron, React, TypeScript, Vite  
**Database:** SQLite  
**AI Integration:** OpenAI API  
**Email Infrastructure:** AWS SES and SNS (integration in progress)

## Development Status

NORIA is under active development.

Core campaign management, AI-assisted composition and safety-related workflows have been implemented or tested in controlled environments.

Production email sending is not currently enabled. AWS SES/SNS integration and production readiness checks remain in progress.
